Three steps, about five minutes

The whole setup is a folder your server already writes to disk. If you can download a file from your host's file manager, you can do this - the setup guide walks through it click by click.

1

Claim your link

Sign in with Discord and name your server. You get a private link like wrapped.gg/servername-k7m2p that is yours for good.

2

Send your stats

In your host's file manager, zip your stats folder and drop it on our uploader. That is world/stats, or world/players/stats on Minecraft 26.X and newer. About 8 MB even for a huge server.

3

Share it

Post the link in your Discord. Everyone types their name and gets their own page, plus a card made for showing off.

What we read, and what we never see

You are handing over other people's data, so it is worth being precise about it.

What we read

  • The stat counters Minecraft already writes to world/stats
  • The same counters in world/players/stats, where Minecraft 26.X and newer put them
  • The name that goes with each UUID, so players can find themselves

What we never see

  • Chat, commands, or logs
  • IP addresses or login times
  • Coordinates, builds, or inventories
  • Your world, your plugins, or anything else on the server

Your link carries a short random code on the end, so nobody finds your server's page by guessing its name. Nothing is public until you press publish, and deleting your server here removes every upload, build and player page with it.

Questions people ask first

If yours is not here, the setup guide goes further.

What is a Minecraft server Wrapped?

It is a year in review for a Minecraft server, in the style of Spotify Wrapped. Minecraft already counts everything each player does - blocks broken, mobs fought, distance walked, deaths - and wrapped.gg turns those counters into a page for every player, ending on a card they can save and post.

How do I get one for my server?

Upload the stats folder your server already writes. Add your server here, zip that folder from your host's file manager and drop it in, or let the one-line shell client send it for you. It takes about five minutes and nothing gets installed on the server.

Do I need a plugin or a mod?

No. There is nothing to install, nothing running alongside the game and no restart. Vanilla, Paper, Spigot, Fabric, Forge, Purpur and every modpack work the same way, because they all write the same stats files.

Is it free?

Yes, for every server and every player, with no limit on how many players a server has. There is no paid tier holding anything back and no card to enter.

Does it work with Bedrock players?

Yes. Bedrock players who join through Geyser and Floodgate are counted like anyone else and get their own page, with the prefix their name carries on your server.

What can you see from my server?

Only the stat counters in the folder you upload, plus the name that goes with each UUID. No chat, no commands, no logs, no IP addresses, no coordinates, no builds and no inventories. Nothing is public until you press publish.
