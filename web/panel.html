Set up your server's Wrapped

wrapped.gg turns the stats folder your Minecraft server already writes into a year in review for every player on it. Four short steps, about five minutes. Nothing is installed on your server, nothing runs alongside the game, and it is free.

Sign in with Discord

Discord only - there is no password to invent and nothing to confirm by email.

This is not a Discord login form

  • The button sends you to discord.com. Check the address bar - if you are ever typing a password somewhere other than Discord's own site, stop.
  • Discord asks you there whether to share your account with wrapped.gg, and sends us a one-time code once you agree.
  • There is no password on wrapped.gg at all, so there is none for us to ask for and none for anyone to steal.

What Discord gives us

  • One scope, identify: your username and avatar, so the panel knows whose servers to show you
  • We cannot read your messages or see which servers you are in
  • We cannot post, join, or change anything on your account
  • We never see your email address or your password

wrapped.gg is run by Tehlo. It is not affiliated with Discord Inc., and not affiliated with Mojang AB or Microsoft. Questions before you sign in are welcome - ask in the support Discord, or read the setup docs first.

Add your stats

In your host's file manager open world, tick the stats folder, press Archive or Compress, and download the file it makes. On Minecraft 26.X and newer that folder is world/players/stats; before that, world/stats. Then drop it below. Show me where that is

Cannot find the folder, or the upload will not take it? Ask in the support Discord - somebody there has seen your panel before.

Run this on your server

Open a terminal on the machine, go to your server's folder - the one with server.properties in it - and paste this. Your key is already in it.

It finds the world on its own, reads the files, and never touches the running server. Safe with players online.

Time window

Minecraft's counters never reset, so one upload covers your whole world. Nominate an earlier upload as the baseline and the Wrapped describes just the period since then instead.

World birthday

The day this world was created. Nothing in a stats folder knows it - world/stats is only written once somebody earns a counter, and the file that does carry a creation date is not part of the upload. So it is yours to set, or to leave blank.

Blocked players

Banned accounts, alts, staff test characters - anyone you would rather your Wrapped did not mention. A blocked player leaves every leaderboard, stops counting towards the server totals and their own page stops answering. Your Wrapped rebuilds from your latest upload the moment you block somebody, so it lands within a minute.
